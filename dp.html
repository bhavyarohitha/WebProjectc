Hospital Departments

Explore our various departments and services

Cardiology

Overview: Focuses on heart-related conditions and treatments.

Services: ECG, Echocardiography, Angioplasty

Neurology

Overview: Specializes in disorders of the nervous system.

Services: EEG, EMG, Neurological examinations

Orthopedics

Overview: Deals with conditions related to bones and joints.

Services: Joint replacement, Fracture care, Sports medicine

Pediatrics

Overview: Provides medical care for infants, children, and adolescents.

Services: Vaccinations, Growth monitoring, Pediatric surgeries

Dermatology

Overview: Focuses on skin, hair, and nail disorders.

Services: Skin cancer screenings, Dermatologic surgery, Acne treatment

Emergency Medicine

Overview: Provides immediate treatment for acute medical conditions.

Services: Trauma care, Acute illness management, Critical care

Gastroenterology

Overview: Focuses on digestive system disorders.

Services: Colonoscopy, Liver function tests, Gastrointestinal surgery

Oncology

Overview: Specializes in cancer treatment and research.

Services: Chemotherapy, Radiation therapy, Surgical oncology

Gynecology

Overview: Focuses on women's health issues.

Services: Annual exams, Menopause management, Obstetric care

Psychiatry

Overview: Deals with mental health disorders.

Services: Therapy, Medication management, Crisis intervention

Urology

Overview: Focuses on urinary tract and male reproductive system disorders.

Services: Kidney stone treatment, Prostate exams, Urological surgeries

Endocrinology

Overview: Specializes in hormone-related disorders.

Services: Diabetes management, Thyroid disorders, Hormone therapy

Radiology

Overview: Uses imaging techniques to diagnose diseases.

Services: X-rays, MRIs, CT scans

Rehabilitation

Overview: Helps patients recover and improve their physical capabilities.

Services: Physical therapy, Occupational therapy, Speech therapy

Allergy & Immunology

Overview: Treats allergic conditions and immune system disorders.

Services: Allergy testing, Immunotherapy, Asthma management
